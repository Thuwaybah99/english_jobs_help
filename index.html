01 / WHY THIS MATTERED

When everything changed, community mattered.

In 2023, war broke out in Sudan and displaced countless people from their homes, communities, and livelihoods. Like many Sudanese, I moved to Egypt and had to start building a new life in an unfamiliar place.

Finding safety was only the beginning. People needed ways to support themselves and their families, yet many were facing a new job market without the knowledge or connections to navigate it.

I knew the uncertainty firsthand. And when I found a way forward, I wanted to share it.

“
I couldn't change what the war had taken from people. But I could share an opportunity that might help them begin again.

03 / TURNING EMPATHY INTO ACTION

Not just job leads.
A chance to start again.

I created a nonprofit community group to support Sudanese people living in Egypt, especially those who had been displaced by the war. I wanted to remove some of the barriers between capable people and the employment opportunities available around them.

HOW IT WORKED

Making the unfamiliar accessible

Through a social media campaign, I introduced Sudanese job seekers to Egypt's English-speaking outsourcing industry, including customer service and sales roles. Many qualified people simply hadn't heard about these openings.

04 / A YEAR OF COMMUNITY SERVICE

Small acts of support.
Meaningful new beginnings.

For a year, I kept sharing opportunities and supporting job seekers. Some found employment; others gained the guidance and confidence to prepare and try again. Every person mattered more than any number.

05 / WHAT STAYED WITH ME

The initiative ended.
The gratitude never did.

Eventually, I had to step away as new life challenges required my attention. But I still think about the people I was able to help during a time when work, stability, and hope were so urgently needed.

It remains one of the most meaningful things I've done. I carry their kindness and prayers with me, and I believe those blessings have stayed in my life.

Sometimes, helping one person find a way forward changes both of your lives.
